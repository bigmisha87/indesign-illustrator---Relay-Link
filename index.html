How it works

Two buttons. That's the whole workflow.

1

Send

In the app where the design lives, choose the whole document or just the selection and press Send. The button takes the colour of where the project is going.

2

Receive

In the other app the package shows up with a green dot. Press Receive to get a new document, or drop it into the one that is open.

The transfer goes through a folder (~/RelayLink-AI-ID by default). Pick another folder in the panel, or open any package with “Open package…”.

What's new

Send exactly what you mean

Three new options in the Send card, and a panel redesigned so you can tell send from receive at a glance. Try them below.

Page range

Only the pages you need

Type ranges and lists, the way you would in a print dialog. 5- means page 5 to the end. Empty means everything.

Pasteboard

Leave the scraps behind

Objects lying around the page stay home. Anything that touches the page, even at the very edge, always goes with it.

4 scraps stay in this file · the orange square touches the page, so it goes
Layers

Keep your layers, or merge them

The other app rebuilds the same layers: names, order, visibility and locks. Switch it off to get everything on one layer, stacked the same way.

What travels

Built for real print and brand work

Pages ⇄ artboards
Document size, every page or artboard, different sizes too, bleed.
Layers
Name, order, visibility, lock. Illustrator sublayers fold into their parent.
Vector shapes
Exact anchor points and handles, compound paths, groups, clipping masks.
Colour
RGB, CMYK, spot and gradients, named swatches, transparency, blend modes, overprint.
Strokes
Weight, caps, joins and dashes.
Type
Font, size, colour, tracking and kerning, leading, alignment, indents, columns, text on a path.
Right-to-left
Hebrew and other right-to-left text keeps its direction and paragraph alignment.
Images
Linked to the same original file, never embedded or copied. Embedded images are saved once next to the document.

What stays behind

Some things have no counterpart in the other app. RelayLink lists every one of them as a warning after each transfer, so nothing disappears silently.

  • Hyperlinks and interactive objects
  • Live effects such as drop shadows
  • Brushes and symbols
  • Tables and footnotes

Install

Ready in a minute

  1. Install both panels

    Open RelayLink-AI-ID_InDesign.zxp and RelayLink-AI-ID_Illustrator.zxp with ZXP Installer.

  2. Restart the apps

    Quit and reopen InDesign and Illustrator so they load the panel.

  3. Open the panel

    Window ▸ Extensions ▸ RelayLink AI-ID, in both apps. Then Send.
